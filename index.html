Technology

One capability, four platforms

Wharton’s jelly MSC platform

Our research team cultures mesenchymal stem cells derived from the Wharton’s jelly of the human umbilical cord. The culture extract developed in-house is officially registered in the International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients (INCI).

  • Wharton’s jelly–derived mesenchymal stem cells
  • INCI-registered stem cell culture extract
  • Adipose-derived stem cell (ADSC) research

Patented exosome isolation

Exosomes are nano-sized vesicles that cells release to carry proteins, lipids and RNA to other cells. Our patented process isolates up to 4×10¹¹ exosome particles per mL by filtration from media collected while co-culturing NK, NKT and T cells.

  • Manufacturing method patent registered, August 2024
  • Filtration-based, high-concentration isolation
  • Exosome ingredients for cosmetic applications

NK and DC-NK-T immune cells

We expand natural killer (NK) cells, innate immune cells that recognise and remove abnormal cells, and culture DC-NK-T combined immune cells, in which dendritic cells, NK cells and T cells are grown together.

  • NK cell expansion
  • DC-NK-T combined immune cell culture
  • NK · NKT · T cell co-culture media

Licensed cell processing

Cell culture and processing take place in a cell processing facility licensed by Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS). We work with a partner clinic designated as an advanced regenerative medicine institution in 2024.

  • MFDS-licensed cell processing facility
  • Partner clinic designated for advanced regenerative medicine
  • Research ethics and safety first
