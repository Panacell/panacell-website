About Panacell

From cell storage to cell culture and application, Panacell Biotech brings the full process under one roof, built on technology developed in-house.

OverviewProfileCertificationsHistoryIdentityLocation
Overview

A regenerative medicine and biomedicine company, with research at the centre.

Panacell Biotech provides a one-stop service that runs from cell storage to cell culture and application. Our work covers activating immune cells, culturing stem cells, and developing cell-derived materials for hair and skin care.

Research and development come first. We bring biotechnology specialists together with clinical partners, so that what we learn in the laboratory can reach patients and customers safely.

Identity

The name

Panacea + Cell

“Pana” comes from panacea, a remedy for all ills. It refers to the ability of stem cells to differentiate in many directions.

Our symbol overlaps the letters P and B for Panacell Biotech, with the molecular structure of a stem cell as its highlight.
